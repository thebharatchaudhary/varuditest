Farmers Oriented

Specialized agro-polymer protective products engineered to enhance farm productivity, preserve fodder nutrition, and provide sustainable organic cultivation.

7 Core Agricultural Solutions
Renewable Bio-Energy

Biogas Tube

Heavy-gauge flexible anaerobic digester tubes and balloon gas holders. Engineered to withstand corrosive methane and hydrogen sulfide gases while providing reliable zero-leak energy for rural homes and dairies.

✓ 100% airtight ultrasonic welded joints ✓ High ambient heat & UV stabilization ✓ Standard 2 m³ to 50 m³ capacities
Crop Yield Protection

Mulching Sheet

UV-stabilized silver-black and black-black multi-layer agricultural film. Blocks sunlight to stop weed growth, saves up to 60% irrigation water by reducing soil evaporation, and prevents soil erosion.

✓ Available in 21, 25, 30 & 50 microns ✓ Standard 400m & 800m continuous rolls ✓ Reduces fertilizer run-off & root rot
Horticulture & Shade

Nursery Sheds

UV-treated high-density polyethylene knitted agro shade nets and greenhouse film. Delivers uniform light diffusion, temperature control, and physical protection against birds, insects, and hail.

✓ 50%, 75% & 90% Shade Factors ✓ Lock-stitch knitting prevents fraying ✓ High chemical & pesticide resistance
High Demand

Azolla Beds

Custom multi-layer waterproof cultivation beds designed for growing Azolla caroliniana (high-protein organic cattle and poultry feed). Fitted with corner anchor channels for easy setup in orchards or yards.

✓ 100% waterproof virgin polymer ✓ Sizes: 10×6×1 ft, 12×4×1 ft, 8×4×1 ft ✓ Enhances cattle milk production naturally
Harvest Quality

Pre-post Harvest Sheets

Heavy-duty floor and cover sheets designed for clean ground threshing, sun-drying of grains/spices, and rapid yard protection during unexpected rainfall. Smooth non-porous surface makes sweeping effortless.

✓ Single panel sizes up to 50 × 50 ft ✓ High tear and grain abrasion resistance ✓ Protects harvest from soil dust & dampness
Livestock Fodder

Silage Cover

Oxygen-barrier silage stretch wraps, bunker covers, and high-tensile silage bags. Facilitates airtight anaerobic lactic acid fermentation to lock in green fodder nutrients for year-round cattle nutrition.

✓ Gas-impermeable multi-layer co-extrusion ✓ UV stabilized for open farm storage ✓ Prevents mold growth & nutrient spoilage
Organic Farming

Vermibeds

BIS-standard HDPE woven vermicomposting beds equipped with net aeration windows for worm respiration and a dedicated drainage outlet to effortlessly harvest pure vermiwash liquid fertilizer.

✓ Standard 12 × 4 × 2 ft & 8 × 4 × 2 ft dimensions ✓ 350-450 GSM reinforced UV fabric ✓ Includes ground fixing peg loops

Vehicle Covers

Heavy commercial fleet covers and precision-tailored covers designed to shield transit trucks, agricultural tractors, and custom equipment from severe weather and transit stress.

About Varudi Enterprise

હવે એ જ અનુભવ, વિશ્વાસ અને ખેડૂતોની જરૂરિયાતોની સમજને આગળ વધારીને Varudi Enterprise નામે એક નવી અને આધુનિક શરૂઆત થઈ.
